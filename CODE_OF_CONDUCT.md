# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We want this project to be welcoming and useful.

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume positive intent
- Focus critique on ideas and behavior, not people
- Keep safety concerns high-priority and specific

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Deliberately suggesting unsafe app-modification steps that violate project safety policy

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, and project discussions.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open a private report to the maintainers with relevant links/context.
